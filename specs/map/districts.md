# Districts

Design notes per district. Numbers (area, landmarks, coordinates) live in `world.yaml`; this file
is about feel, function, and gameplay.

---

## Colón — `colon` (5 km²)

**Feel:** Faded grandeur next to fenced-off wealth. Arcaded timber and concrete tenements, laundry
on balconies, the sea at the end of every street; then a wall, and behind it the Free Zone's
warehouses and the port's cranes.

**Zones:** container port (north shore) · Free Zone (walled grid, gates) · old town grid · Atlantic
locks (west).

**Gameplay:** Neto's home turf. Crane operation, boat launches, Free Zone infiltration, the renewal
eviction storyline. Port security is the main police presence.

**Signature moment:** a ship rising in the Atlantic locks at sunset, seen from Neto's crane cab.

---

## Gatún Lake — `gatun-lake` (8 km²)

**Feel:** Flat green water, drowned forest stumps, jungle islands, ships moving slowly in a line.

**Gameplay:** Boat travel, hiding, a smuggling-route side activity. Ships in the channel are huge
moving obstacles.

**Signature moment:** a container ship passing within meters of a small launch at night.

---

## Jungle ridge — `jungle-ridge` (7 km²)

**Feel:** Dense rainforest, fog in the mornings, howler monkeys. The highway and railroad cut
through it side by side.

**Gameplay:** The isthmus crossing. High-speed highway chases, a train you can board or race,
dirt tracks for off-road escapes. Low-cost to build: mostly terrain and vegetation scatter.

**Signature moment:** the ridge overlook — the one place where both oceans are on screen.

---

## Central canal — `canal-central` (3 km²)

**Feel:** Former canal-zone village, wooden houses on stilts, dredges and tugs, the cut through the
hills, a white cable-stayed bridge overhead.

**Gameplay:** Quiet hub between the two halves of the map; a safehouse candidate; river and canal
boat routes.

---

## Pacific locks and Balboa — `pacific-locks` (4 km²)

**Feel:** Industrial and touristy side by side: lock gates and a visitor center, the Pacific
container terminal, the bus terminal and mall, the arch bridge at the canal entrance, the causeway
out into the bay.

**Gameplay:** Transit hub (bus, rail terminus), port jobs, chases across the arch bridge.

**Signature moment:** a chase across the arch bridge with a ship passing underneath.

---

## City core — `city-core` (5 km²)

**Feel:** Three cities in one: the restored colonial old quarter, the bayfront boulevard and park,
and the glass towers of the banking district. Traffic jams, constant construction, rain at 4 pm.

**Zones:** old quarter (south peninsula) · coastal avenue · banking towers · El Cangrejo-style
residential district (north).

**Gameplay:** Valeria's home turf. Office infiltration, forgery and registry missions, traffic-heavy
driving, rooftop views.

**Signature moment:** the firm tower at night, lit floor by floor, seen from the old quarter's
seawall.

---

## Costa del Este — `costa-del-este` (2 km²)

**Feel:** Gated, landscaped, quiet, private security on every corner. Corporate HQs and luxury
towers on reclaimed land.

**Gameplay:** Where the antagonists live. High-security stealth, private-guard response instead of
police.

---

## The corridor — `tito-corridor` (5 km²)

**Feel:** The city most visitors only see from a car on the way to the airport. Hills packed with
homes, steep stairs, corner stores, street markets, buses, music, planes low overhead every few
minutes.

**Zones:** hills (north, steep and maze-like) · market and bus stops · Tito's block under the flight
path.

**Gameplay:** Tito's home turf. Motorbike routes through alleys and stairs, delivery side-jobs,
barrio reputation per neighborhood, rooftop escapes.

**Representation note:** this district is a community, not a crime zone. Most of its people are
workers like Tito; its crews are part of it, not all of it.

**Signature moment:** a night delivery run as a plane comes in overhead, everyone looking up.

---

## Tocumen airport — `airport` (2 km²)

**Feel:** Two runways, a passenger terminal, and on the far side the cargo terminal: floodlit apron,
tugs and belt loaders, fences and badge gates.

**Gameplay:** Airside stealth on Tito's shifts, the final-act cargo heist, a runway chase.

**Signature moment:** a ground vehicle racing a taxiing cargo plane down the apron.

---

## Open sea — `sea` (7 km²)

Caribbean in the north, Bay of Panama in the south, with anchored ships waiting to enter the canal.
Boats only; map edge is soft (current pushes you back).
